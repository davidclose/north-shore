Where this comes from — Weather and tide come from the free Open-Meteo forecast and refresh every time you open the page, every 15 minutes while it stays open, and whenever you press Refresh. Ships come live from your aisstream.io AIS feed: the page listens for about five minutes after you open it, then checks again every few minutes. Moving ships show up within seconds; ships at anchor or moored only report every three minutes, so they can take a little longer. Ship photos are freely licensed pictures from Wikimedia Commons, shown only when the match to the ship is clear. Your feed key stays on the server and never reaches this page.
